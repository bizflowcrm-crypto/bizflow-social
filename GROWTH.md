# BizFlow Instagram Growth Plan

Goal: grow @bizflowindia followers and engagement with a witty, relatable brand
voice (the way Zomato does it), aimed at small business owners in Maharashtra.

## 1. The voice: "तुमचा दुकानातला मित्र"

BizFlow talks like a funny friend who also runs a shop, not like a software company.

- Jokes come from **daily dukandar life**: udhaar, "पुढच्या आठवड्यात देतो", month-end GST,
  stock guessing, late-night hisab, chai, festival rush, UPI "sir payment झालं का?".
- Marathi first, everyday English words mixed in. Short lines. One joke per post.
- Laugh **with** shop owners, never at them. Never mock customers, competitors,
  religion, caste, politics or any community.
- Every funny post still ends with one soft line about BizFlow or a question.
- Ideas are our own. Never copy another brand's posts, memes or tagline.

Every caption ends with a **question or a "tag a friend" line**. Comments and shares
are what make Instagram show a post to non-followers.

## 3. Commenting on other accounts (you do this, 10 minutes a day)

Automated commenting gets accounts blocked, and no tool is allowed to do it.
The big brands do it by hand. So: the autopilot writes fresh lines each week
(in `reports/`), and a person at BizFlow posts them.

**Daily routine**
1. Open Instagram at ~10:30 AM and ~7 PM.
2. Comment on **5 fresh posts** (posted in the last hour works best).
3. Reply to **every** comment and DM on BizFlow's own posts, within an hour if possible.

**Where to comment (in this order)**
1. **Sangamner / Ahilyanagar / Nashik local pages and influencers** — your best post
   ever came from local influencers. This is where future customers are.
2. **Local shops, restaurants, hotels** that post regularly — they are your buyers.
   Be a fan first; never pitch in comments.
3. **Marathi meme and business pages**, CA / GST educators.
4. **Big Indian brands** with funny comment sections (food delivery, payments, quick
   commerce). A witty comment under a viral post gets seen by thousands.

**Comment starters** (adapt to the post, never paste the same line twice)

Under a local shop or restaurant post:
- "हे पाहून भूक लागली आणि बिल बनवायची इच्छा पण 😄 मस्त!"
- "संगमनेरचा अभिमान 🙌 असंच वाढत रहा!"
- "गर्दी पाहून आनंद झाला. हिशोब आमच्यावर सोडा 😉"

Under a food delivery / quick commerce brand post:
- "तुम्ही 10 मिनिटांत delivery करता, आम्ही 10 सेकंदात बिल 😎"
- "Order तुमची, बिल आमचं. Deal? 🤝"

Under a payments / UPI brand post:
- "Payment तुम्ही करा, ‘बाकी किती?’ ते आम्ही सांगतो 😌"
- "‘Sir, payment झालं का?’ हा प्रश्न कायमचा बंद करूया 🤝"

Under a GST / finance educator post:
- "हे save केलं. महिनाअखेरीस कामाला येणार 🔖"
- "इतकं सोपं कोणी सांगितलंच नव्हतं 👏"

Under a meme about business, Monday, or month-end:
- "आमच्या customers चा रोजचा किस्सा 😂"
- "Month-end आणि दुकानदार: एक भावनिक नातं 🥲"

Under a festival post from a local page:
- "शुभेच्छा! यंदा दुकानात गर्दी आणि वहीत शांतता राहो 🙏😄"

**Rules for comments**
- No phone number, no "DM us", no link. A good comment makes people tap the profile.
- Never comment under a competitor's post. Never argue. Never comment on news,
  tragedy, politics or religion.
- Max ~5 comments in one sitting, spread through the day.

## 4. Follower growth moves

1. **Collab posts with Sangamner influencers.** The visarjan post reached 8x more than
   a product post. Invite 1–2 local creators a month, and publish as an Instagram
   Collab so it shows on both profiles.
2. **Customer spotlights.** Photo of a real shop using BizFlow, or a website we built,
   + one line from the owner, tag their account. They share it, their customers see you.
   500+ businesses use BizFlow; the named ones are in `CUSTOMERS.md` (30+ websites, and shops, traders and factories
   using AccountFlow, and hotels and bars using TableFlow, and shops using RetailFlow, across Sangamner, Kopargaon, Pune and nearby). Ask the customer before featuring them.
3. **Fix the bio.** One line on what you do in Marathi, "Free demo 👇", link to
   bizflowindia.cloud, and a WhatsApp button.
4. **Stories every day.** Polls and question stickers (must be added in the app):
   "वही की app?", "GST भरताना सगळ्यात मोठी अडचण?".
5. **Festival offer or giveaway** around Dussehra and Diwali: "follow + tag a
   business friend". Write clear rules, pick the winner publicly.
6. **Reels on YouTube Shorts too** — already automatic.
7. **Real faces.** Team and office clips do better than graphics. Put photos or short
   clips in `photos/` and the autopilot builds posts around them.
